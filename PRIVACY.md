# Privacy Policy

**CatSwitch** is a Windows desktop app that updates your Twitch stream category and title based on what you're playing.

There is no CatSwitch server, no account system, and no analytics or telemetry.

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Your data

All your settings, lists and data are saved to your local app data folder and never shared with anyone. Your Twitch access token is encrypted and can only be used on this device, under this Windows account.

Game detection runs completely local and does not send or collect any data.

## Third-party services

CatSwitch connects to the following third-party services. When you use the features that rely on them, your data is handled by that service under its own privacy policy, linked in each section below.

## Twitch

Twitch is used to authenticate your account, to load and update your stream category and title, and to fetch category data such as box art. Signing in stores an access token on your PC so you stay logged in; category and title changes are sent to Twitch when the app applies them. These requests share the same basic connection information any web request does, such as your IP address. You can revoke CatSwitch's access at any time under Connections in your Twitch settings.

Twitch's privacy policy applies to your use of Twitch:
https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/

## Discord

When Discord's detected games list is enabled, the app accesses the Discord API to retrieve a public list of detectable applications, which helps reduce false positives when telling games apart from other software. No Discord account or login is required and nothing about you is sent, beyond the same basic connection information any web request shares, such as your IP address.

Discord's privacy policy applies to your use of Discord:
https://discord.com/privacy

## GitHub

GitHub is used to check for available updates and to download them when you choose to install one. No account is required, and nothing is sent beyond the same basic connection information any web request shares, such as your IP address.

GitHub's privacy policy applies to your use of GitHub:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Deleting your data

To delete your data, choose the delete data option in the uninstaller. This clears everything the app created and saved, including settings, lists, themes, caches and your Twitch token. Uninstalling without that option leaves your data in place so a reinstall can pick up where you left off, and updates never touch it. You can also delete the app data folder yourself while the app is closed.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are made in this repository and the "Last updated" date above will change.
